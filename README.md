# McCormick & Co (MKC) - Leveraged Buyout Model (LBO)

An independent LBO model on McCormick & Company, built in Excel to model a
HYPOTHETICAL take-private of the business.

## Summary
- **Entry:** 12.0x LTM EBITDA (~$1,071m) → ~$12.9bn enterprise value
- **Leverage:** ~6.0x total (Term Loan + PIK), revolver undrawn at close
- **Hold:** 5 years, exit at 12.0x
- **Returns:** ~2.5x MoM / ~20% IRR - driven by EBITDA growth and debt paydown, not EBITDA multiple increase

## What the model does
- **Sources & Uses** with purchase-price allocation and goodwill creation
  (equity step-up, asset write-up, deferred tax liability)
- **Multi-tranche debt** (Term Loan, PIK, revolver) with an FCF waterfall:
  mandatory amortisation → cash sweep → optional prepayment
- **Integrated P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow** with financing-fee
  amortisation, non-cash PIK interest accrual, and DTL unwind
- **Pre- and post-transaction balance sheet** showing the deal adjustments
- **Exit proceeds waterfall** (management option pool, sponsor/rollover split)
  driving MoM and IRR
- **Sensitivity analysis** on entry vs. exit multiple
